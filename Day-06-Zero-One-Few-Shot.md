# Day 6 - Zero-shot, One-shot & Few-shot

## What is Zero-shot?

I don't show the AI any examples.(Zero-shot means asking the AI to do a task without giving any examples.)

Example - "Write an email."

## What is One-shot?

I have to show the AI any one examples.(One-shot means giving the AI one example before asking it to do the task.)

Example -"Here's one email I wrote. Write the next one in the same style."
                

## What is Few-shot?
                 
I have to show the AI two or more examples.

Eample - "Here are three emails I've written. Write the fourth one in the same style."
